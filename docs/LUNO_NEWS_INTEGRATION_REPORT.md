# LUNO News Integration Report

## 1. News Tab & Article Reader (Screenshot 5)
- **Hero Featured Card**: Full-bleed thumbnail with gradient overlay, headline (`단독 "SKY 학벌 중시하는 서울, 삶의 질은 세계 113위"`), press name (`매일경제`), and carousel indicator dots.
- **Category Filtering**: Instant filtering across `전체`, `인기`, `경제`, `IT/과학`, `글로벌`, `엔터` via `filteredPopularNews`.
- **Ranked Popular Articles**: Numbered badges (`1`–`4`), 80×60px rounded thumbnails, 2-line clamped headlines, and press metadata.
- **Article Reader & Chat Sharing**: Clicking any article opens the styled reader modal with bookmark toggling and one-click sharing (`💬 대화방으로 공유하기`) directly into the active conversation composer.
