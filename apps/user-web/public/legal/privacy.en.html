LUNO Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-21.1 · Effective 2026-11-21

1. What we process and why

PurposeData
Identification and sign-inEmail, password (PBKDF2 hash only), display name
Service deliveryChats (1:1, group and Keep rooms are end-to-end encrypted and unreadable to us), friends, reports, support tickets, error/perf telemetry (sensitive values masked)
Age checkBirthdate — verified at signup, then destroyed immediately, never stored

2. Retention and deletion

Account data is deleted promptly after withdrawal. Records a law requires us to keep are held only for that period.

3. Children

We do not accept users under 14. Accounts found to belong to children are removed after review.

4. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of your data via Settings > Support.

5. Sharing and transfers

We do not share your data with third parties by default. Any cross-border transfer will be announced with prior consent.
